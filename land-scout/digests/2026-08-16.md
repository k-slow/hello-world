## Coverage Note

This run was WebSearch-only — deep fetch (WebFetch) was blocked by the environment's egress policy, so tier-2 sources never loaded: crexi.com, loopnet.com, landsearch.com, and Kane/McHenry/Cook GIS and assessor parcel viewers were all unreachable, along with roughly 25 municipal, county, state and utility domains including huntley.il.us, hampshireil.org, elginil.gov, mchenrycountyedc.com, dceo.illinois.gov, comed.com and nicorgas.com.

Direct consequence: every utility reading above is **claimed by the listing or village, none independently verified** against a provider or GIS record. That capped the utilities-completeness score at 12 of 20 points for all 16 sites — the reason no site reached Grade A this run is a measurement ceiling, not a judgment that the corridor lacks A-grade land.

FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer and the USFWS National Wetlands Inventory were also unreachable; floodplain/wetland risk above is drawn from search evidence only, and is explicitly unchecked on 1900 N LaFox St (Fox River proximity) and Prairie Stone Parcel 20 (park has documented wetlands).

Separately: no Kane/McHenry land bank exists in the tax-reversion sense, and DCEO Vetted Sites/Megasites, Intersect Illinois, ComEd and Nicor econdev programs returned only program descriptions, no in-corridor parcels in the 3.5–15 ac band.

**Rejections (15 of 31 evaluated).** 2 failed on geography, 5 on acreage, 7 on sanitary sewer, 1 was an already-committed building rather than vacant land. Two are worth flagging because they show the filter is doing real work: a Sleepy Hollow listing advertising "right off Highway 255" was rejected outright — I-255 is a Metro East route roughly 250 miles south and does not exist in northern Illinois. And an 18-acre parcel marketed as "Pingree Grove" next to the Otter Creek Shopping Center is actually in Elgin (Otter Creek sits at 200–300 S Randall Rd, Elgin 60123); Randall Rd does not run through Pingree Grove, roughly 6 miles west, so jurisdiction and therefore water/sewer/zoning could not be established. All 15 rejections, with reasons, are logged to the ledger so they will not resurface in tomorrow's run.

**Correction.** Sutton Crossing's zoning was initially read by the scout as "office and industrial." On review it is actually B-2 Community Business and O-3 Office and Research Tollway District, master-planned for 2.2M SF of retail, office and hotel — reflected correctly in that card above.

**Run notes.** Run completed successfully end-to-end; all scheduled scout and underwriter queries executed and all 16 qualified sites cleared the report threshold (55), so nothing was withheld or truncated (16 of a 25-per-email cap). The degradation described above is an environment-level fetch restriction, not a scout failure — treat every "claimed" utility and unconfirmed flood/wetland status as open due-diligence items, not settled facts.

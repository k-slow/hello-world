# Fund Outreach Schedule

A daily operating rhythm built around **Eat the Frog** mornings and a
disciplined afternoon follow-up window. Two focused outreach blocks per day,
gym in the early morning, hard stops to protect energy.

## Guiding principles

1. **Frog first.** The hardest, highest-stakes outreach (new intros, cold
   asks, the LP you've been avoiding) happens in the AM block. No email,
   no Slack, no inbox triage until the frog is dead.
2. **Two windows, no more.** Outreach is bursty work; spreading it across
   the day kills focus and produces low-quality touches. Batch it.
3. **Volume target beats vibes.** Motivation follows action. Hit the daily
   number even on bad days — the streak is the point.
4. **Every touch logged.** If it isn't in the tracker, it didn't happen.
   You can't follow up on what you can't see.

## Daily schedule (M–F)

| Time            | Block                         | Purpose                                                    |
| --------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 5:00 – 8:00 AM  | Gym + breakfast               | Move the body, clear the head, no phone for outreach       |
| 8:00 – 9:00 AM  | **AM Outreach (Frog Block)**  | New intros, cold reach-outs, the one ask you're dreading   |
| 9:00 – 12:00 PM | Deep work / meetings          | Diligence, deck work, calls                                |
| 12:00 – 1:00 PM | Lunch + walk                  | Hard break — no laptop                                     |
| 1:00 – 4:00 PM  | Deep work / meetings          | Whatever the day demands                                   |
| 4:00 – 5:00 PM  | **PM Outreach (Follow-ups)**  | Nudges, replies, scheduling, sending materials, CRM hygiene |
| 5:00 PM onward  | Off                           | Protect it                                                 |

## AM block (8–9 AM) — the Frog

Goal: **3 new outbound touches** before 9:00 AM.

Order of operations:
1. **(0–5 min)** Open the tracker. Identify the 3 hardest names you'd
   rather skip. Those are the frogs.
2. **(5–45 min)** Write and send. One paragraph. Personal hook, the ask,
   a specific next step (15-min intro call this week or next).
3. **(45–60 min)** Log each touch in the tracker with date, channel,
   next-action date.

Hard rules:
- No inbox before the 3 touches are sent.
- Drafts are not touches. Sent counts.
- If you finish early, queue tomorrow's 3 frogs and stop.

## PM block (4–5 PM) — Follow-ups

Goal: **clear every follow-up due today** + send 2–3 nudges on stale threads.

Order of operations:
1. **(0–10 min)** Pull the tracker filter: "Next action ≤ today".
2. **(10–45 min)** Work the list top-to-bottom. Reply, send materials,
   schedule calls, push next-action dates.
3. **(45–60 min)** 7-day stale sweep — anyone who's gone quiet gets a
   short, low-pressure nudge.

## Weekly cadence

| Day       | Theme                                                                 |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Monday    | New intros — biggest batch of frogs of the week                       |
| Tuesday   | Warm intros + asking existing LPs/contacts for referrals              |
| Wednesday | Mid-week follow-up push; clear anything aging past 5 days             |
| Thursday  | New intros round 2 + materials follow-ups                             |
| Friday    | Pipeline review (30 min, 4:00–4:30), then light follow-ups, then off  |

## Targets

Set a number, watch the streak.

- **15 new outbound touches / week** (3/day × 5 days)
- **All same-day follow-ups cleared** by 5 PM
- **0 threads stale > 10 days** by Friday close
- **1 pipeline review / week** (Fri 4:00–4:30)

## Tracker fields (minimum)

If you don't already have a sheet/CRM, start here:

- Name
- Firm
- Channel (email / LinkedIn / intro)
- First touch date
- Last touch date
- Status (Cold / Engaged / In diligence / Committed / Passed)
- **Next action**
- **Next action date**

The two bold fields are the whole game. Everything else is metadata.

## Motivation rules

- **The 2-minute rule.** Can't start? Open the doc and write the salutation.
  That's it. Momentum follows.
- **Streak counter.** Mark an X on the calendar for every day you hit
  3 frogs. Don't break the chain.
- **Reward the reps, not the outcomes.** You don't control replies. You
  control sends. Celebrate sends.
- **One bad day is fine. Two is a pattern.** If you miss the AM block,
  do 5 frogs the next morning, not 0.

## First-week ramp

| Day | AM frogs | Notes                                              |
| --- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Mon | 2        | Lower the bar to start the streak                  |
| Tue | 2        | Same                                               |
| Wed | 3        | Step up                                            |
| Thu | 3        | Hold                                               |
| Fri | 3 + review | Friday review locks in the pattern for next week |

After week 1, hold at 3/day and reassess monthly — bump to 4 if it feels
easy, or rebalance toward follow-up quality if the pipeline is getting
crowded.

Follow-Ups from the Management Discussion

Benefit Street Partners · prepared with Union Square Advisors · July 2026
Thank you again to the TCN team for a substantive session. Two follow-ups would help us complete our work: the product demonstration the team suggested as a separate session, and a short list of written items. We would appreciate scheduling the demonstration this week if the team's calendar allows.

1Product demonstration session (~45 minutes, this week)

  • A live AI Voice Agent session on the production environment: an end-to-end collections interaction including compliance disclosures, a negotiation or payment-plan turn, and escalation to a human agent. Unscripted interaction would be most useful.
  • A brief live configuration walk-through: standing up a simple agent (e.g., KYC) as described on the call, so we can see the "configuration, not code" motion and typical time-to-live first-hand.
  • Early performance data from production deployments: containment / automation rate and cost per resolution, however preliminary.
  • A short SmartAMD demonstration in the same session: live dials against an iOS device with call screening enabled, a Pixel with Call Screen, and a standard voicemail box, showing the classification and resulting action in real time, together with the reporting view and how the uplift metrics are measured.

2Written items

  • Retention bridge: gross revenue retention for the trailing 8 quarters, split churn vs. downsell, by segment or cohort, reflecting the upsell/downsell dynamics discussed on the call.
  • ARR composition: committed minimums / platform fees vs. volume-based billing, as of Q2 2026.
  • Virtual agent funnel: the ~150 evaluating / 8-15 implementing / production customers referenced on the call, with associated ARR, use cases, and go-live dates (anonymized profiles are fine).
  • SmartAMD: the cohort data behind the ~$600K ARR pace and the uplift metrics cited (talk-off lift, screened-call recovery, collections impact), plus current attach and pricing.
  • Technology stack: confirmation of which voice-agent components are in-house models vs. licensed engines (in particular speech-to-text and text-to-speech), and current LLM vendor arrangements.
  • AI organization: who leads AI product and engineering day to day, senior AI hires and backgrounds, and AI-dedicated headcount within the engineering organization.
  • AI roadmap: the 12-24 month roadmap with expected timing and owners, including the predictive-optimization and real-time audio intelligence initiatives referenced in the CIP.
  • Third-party agents: the number of customers running third-party virtual agents through the TCN platform today, and how TCN participates commercially in those interactions.

Management Call: Agenda & Advance Requests

Benefit Street Partners · via Union Square Advisors · 60-90 minutes · requested this week · please include the CTO and product lead
Five topics, in priority order, plus one requirement: a live demonstration of the AI Voice Agent. Advance data requests are listed at the bottom so the discussion can focus on interpretation rather than retrieval.

1Retention quality

Reconcile ~98% dollar logo retention with 81% gross revenue retention: walk through the bridge (churn vs downsell), by segment and cohort. What is driving the downsell in accounts that stay? What share of ARR sits on committed minimums vs pure volume-variable billing?

2Pricing model and AI economics

The model prices AI Voice at $0.20-0.50 per minute against ~$0.05 human-led. How durable is that premium as voice-AI market prices deflate, and is outcome-based pricing (per resolution) on the roadmap? What does TCN monetize if a customer runs a third-party AI agent on its own telephony?

3AI roadmap credibility

Shipped vs planned, with dates and owners: the AI Voice Agent ramp from the April 2026 first customer; "predictive optimization" and "real-time audio intelligence" (spec, training data, delivery dates); the proprietary-model inventory beyond SmartAMD; and, given FCC constraints on outbound automation, the strategy for inbound conversion as the near-term AI opportunity.

4Implementation and services

Who configures and deploys AI agents for a mid-market collections customer? Describe the forward-deployed / professional-services capability: headcount, cost per deployment, time-to-live, and how it scales across 1,000+ accounts that cannot self-configure.

5Live demonstration (required)

A live, not pre-recorded, end-to-end AI Voice Agent collections call: compliance disclosures, negotiation / promise-to-pay, and human escalation. Alongside it: the live customer's containment rate and cost-per-resolution to date.

Advance data requests (before the call)
  • GRR bridge for the last 8 quarters: gross churn vs downsell, split by segment/cohort.
  • ARR split: committed minimums / platform fees vs volume-variable, as of Q2 2026.
  • Agentic AI status as of June 30, 2026: live customers, ARR, pipeline.
  • Current price sheet for AI SKUs (Voice Bot, chat bots, SmartAMD).

Management Discussion: Proposed Agenda

Benefit Street Partners · prepared with Union Square Advisors · 60-90 minutes
Thank you in advance for making time. We would appreciate scheduling at the team's earliest availability this week, and it would be very helpful to have TCN's technology and product leadership on the call alongside the executive team. Five discussion topics are proposed below, together with a short list of materials that would make the conversation most productive if shared in advance.

1Revenue retention

A walk-through of the gross revenue retention bridge (gross churn vs. downsell), by segment and cohort, and the drivers of downsell within retained accounts. We would also like to understand the mix of committed minimums / platform fees vs. volume-based billing within ARR.

2Pricing model and AI economics

The pricing philosophy behind the AI Voice offering, expectations for AI voice pricing over the plan period in light of market trends, any plans for outcome-based pricing, and how TCN thinks about monetization across the range of ways customers may adopt AI (including third-party solutions).

3AI product roadmap

The AI roadmap with timing and ownership: the AI Voice Agent ramp following the first customer deployment; the products described as in development (predictive optimization, real-time audio intelligence); the proprietary model portfolio; and how TCN is prioritizing inbound vs. outbound automation given the regulatory posture around outbound.

4Implementation and customer success

How AI deployments are delivered for mid-market customers: the implementation / professional-services model, typical time-to-live and effort per deployment, and how that motion scales across the installed base.

5Product demonstration

We would ask the team to include a live demonstration of the AI Voice Agent handling a collections interaction end to end (including compliance disclosures and escalation to a human agent), together with early performance data from live deployments (e.g., containment and cost-per-resolution). A live session, rather than a recording, would be most useful for our purposes.

Materials requested in advance
  • Gross revenue retention bridge for the trailing 8 quarters (churn vs. downsell, by segment).
  • ARR composition: committed minimums / platform fees vs. volume-based, as of Q2 2026.
  • Agentic AI status as of June 30, 2026: live customers, associated ARR, and pipeline.
  • Current rate card for AI products (AI Voice, chat automation, SmartAMD).

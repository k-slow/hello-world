AI & Technology Moat — Signal vs. Sell-Side

TOBenefit Street Partners — Investment Committee FROMK. Slowinski — Independent AI & Technology Advisor RETCN (Project Teton): does the AI constitute a real moat? DATEJune 2026  ·  Strictly Private & Confidential
Bottom line up front

TCN is a good business with no AI moat. The CIP and the Palo Alto commercial VDD sell an "AI + compliance moat" the technology does not support. Fund the franchise; price the AI as an option, not a fact.

What's real — fund this

  • ~$60M ARR, ~30%+ organic growth, ~24% EBITDA — a bootstrapped Rule-of-60. Genuinely attractive.
  • Sticky: ~98% logo retention and deep outbound-dialer switching costs.
  • Consumption pricing (not per-seat) — well-positioned to monetize AI usage without cannibalizing itself. This is the single best part of the story.

What's hype — don't pay for it

  • "AI moat." Exactly one proprietary model: SmartAMD, an answering-machine detector. The generative AI (transcription, summaries, QA) runs on GPT / Gemini / Whisper — rented, not owned. Agentic voice = one customer, ~$1M ARR. A launch, not a moat.
  • "Compliance moat." Table stakes. LiveVox, Five9, Convoso and AI-native Skit.ai all market it; it's buyable à la carte (DNC.com, Gryphon). The AI-native entrants are compliance-native too.
  • The Palo Alto VDD grades nearly every finding maximum-positive. That is a second marketing deck, not independent diligence.
  • "$14.4B AI TAM" assumes 100% AI-agent adoption across all verticals. A ceiling dressed as an opportunity.
The risk nobody priced

The entire bull case assumes AI keeps flowing through TCN's dialer. If customers adopt AI-native agents (Decagon, Sierra, Skit.ai) that bring their own telephony, TCN is disintermediated into commodity pipes. Neither document stress-tests this — it is the gating technical-diligence question.

Recommendation

  • Underwrite the ~$60M franchise on switching costs + consumption pricing + last-independent-in-a-niche (post-LiveVox/NICE). Treat AI as optionality, not thesis.
  • Do not pay an AI premium. Make one question gate the tech DD: does TCN stay in the call path as AI agents replace human seats?

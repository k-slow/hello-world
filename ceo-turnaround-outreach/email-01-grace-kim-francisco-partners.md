## Why this version

- **Subject line does the work.** "Trilogy" + "highest EBITDA margin AND revenue-per-employee" is the one-two punch a PE talent partner cares about most: profit and operational efficiency in a single sentence. Earns the click in under a second.
- **Opening sentence repeats both proof points** so even a 5-second scan transmits the signal. Revenue-per-employee is the internal metric PE firms use when sizing up turnaround candidates: it signals you can right-size a bloated org without breaking it.
- **Second paragraph names FP's thesis back to them.** "Buying distressed software and rebuilding them" is exactly how the firm describes itself. Mirroring their language signals you're not spraying. Then "what I enjoy and what I'm built for" closes the loop: this is a choice, not a job hunt.
- **No team mention, no attachment, no portfolio name-drops.** All of that comes out on the call when she asks.
- **"20 minutes" + calendar link + "two windows"** gives her two zero-friction ways to say yes.

## Tactical notes

- **Send 7:30–8:00 AM Pacific Monday.** Grace is San Francisco-based per FP's site.
- **No LinkedIn message same day.** Wait 48 hours. If no email reply by Wednesday AM, send a one-line LinkedIn note: *"Sent you a note Monday on a turnaround CEO conversation - happy to resend if it didn't land."*
- **If she replies:** Have your one-pager ready (1 page, top half = Trilogy results, bottom half = turnaround thesis + senior team composition). Send it within 30 minutes of her reply.
- **If she punts to a colleague (likely Brittany Stewart, Sr. Operating Advisor, Executive Talent):** Take the meeting with Stewart - she's also a real decision node, not a deflection.
- **What to expect on the call:** She'll ask (a) reference list, (b) financial outcome at Trilogy with specifics including the EBITDA-margin and revenue-per-employee numbers, (c) what kind of distressed situations you're best at (operational vs. strategic vs. financial), (d) what you walk away from versus what you'd take on. Have crisp answers ready.

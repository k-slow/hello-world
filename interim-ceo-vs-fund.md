# Interim CEO vs. Running a $150M Fund — The Math

Back-of-envelope economic comparison. Numbers are market-typical ranges (US, 2024-26), not promises. Tax treatment is pre-tax unless noted.

---

## Path A — Serial Interim CEO

**Per-engagement comp (PE-backed or distressed turnaround):**

| Component | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly retainer | $40k – $80k | Or $3k–$8k day rate |
| Engagement length | 9 – 15 months | |
| Cash per engagement | $400k – $1.2M | Retainer × duration |
| Completion / success bonus | $250k – $1.0M | Tied to milestone (sale, refi, EBITDA) |
| Equity grant | 0.5% – 2.0% of equity | Often pro-rata vest, sometimes accelerated on exit |

**Annualized assumptions (steady-state operator who keeps the pipeline full):**

- ~1 engagement at a time, ~75% utilization across the year
- Expected cash: **$750k – $1.5M / yr**
- Equity hit rate: maybe 1-in-3 engagements produces a meaningful payout
  - Hit payout range: $500k – $5M
  - Expected value: **~$300k – $1M / yr** (highly lumpy)

**10-year cumulative (mid-case): ~$10M – $20M, mostly W-2 / 1099 cash.**

Capital at risk: **$0.** Reputation at risk: high.

## Path B — Solo / Lead GP of a $150M Fund

Assumes 2/20 with 8% hurdle, 5-year investment period + 5-year harvest, GP commit 1.5%.

### Fund-level economics (gross)

| Year | Mgmt fee basis | Fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 – 5 | 2.0% on $150M committed | $3.0M / yr | Investment period |
| 6 – 10 | 1.5% on invested capital (~$130M, decaying) | $1.5M – $2.0M / yr | Steps down |
| **Cumulative** | | **~$23M – $26M over fund life** | |

### Run the firm out of the fee

Annual operating expenses for a $150M fund:

| Line | Cost |
|---|---|
| 1 analyst + 1 associate + 1 ops/CFO-lite | $900k – $1.4M loaded |
| Office, tech, data, travel | $200k – $400k |
| Legal, audit, fund admin, tax | $300k – $500k |
| Insurance (E&O, D&O), compliance | $100k – $200k |
| **Total opex** | **$1.5M – $2.5M / yr** |

**Net mgmt-fee profit to GP partners:**
- Years 1–5: ~$0.5M – $1.5M / yr
- Years 6–10: $0 – $0.5M / yr (after step-down)
- Cumulative net fee profit: **~$3M – $9M over 10 yrs**

### Carry (the real prize)

| Scenario | Gross MOIC | Profit | Pref hurdle (8%) | Carry pool (20%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Below median | 1.5x | $75M | ~$75M | **~$0** (right at hurdle) |
| Median | 2.0x | $150M | ~$75M | **~$15M** |
| Top quartile | 2.5x | $225M | ~$75M | **~$30M** |
| Top decile | 3.0x | $300M | ~$75M | **~$45M** |

Carry is taxed as LTCG (~20% fed + state). Pays out years 6–10 as exits hit.

If you're the solo / lead GP keeping 60–80% of carry: **$0 – $36M depending on outcome.**

### GP commit

You write a check of **~$2.25M** (1.5% of $150M) into your own fund — illiquid for 7–10 years.

### Total to lead GP, 10 years

| Outcome | Net fees | Carry (60% share) | GP commit return @ MOIC | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fund flops (1.5x) | $4M | $0 | $3.4M (on $2.25M in) | **~$5M net of commit** |
| Median (2.0x) | $5M | $9M | $4.5M | **~$16M net of commit** |
| Top quartile (2.5x) | $6M | $18M | $5.6M | **~$27M net of commit** |
| Top decile (3.0x) | $6M | $27M | $6.75M | **~$37M net of commit** |

## Side-by-side

| Factor | Interim CEO | $150M Fund |
|---|---|---|
| Year-1 cash to you | $750k – $1.5M | $500k – $1.5M (after opex) |
| Capital at risk | $0 | ~$2.25M, locked 7–10 yrs |
| Time horizon to know if it worked | 1 year per gig | 5–7 years |
| 10-yr expected take, mid-case | $12M – $18M | $14M – $18M |
| 10-yr upside, top decile | $20M – $30M | $35M+ |
| 10-yr downside | Pipeline goes dry → $0; reputation intact | Fund underperforms → ~$5M, no Fund II, "ex-GP" tag |
| Time on fundraising / LP servicing | 0% | 30–50% |
| Optionality | High — can exit any cycle | Locked in for the decade |
| Tax mix | Mostly ordinary income | Carry as LTCG, fees as ordinary |
| Scalability | Linear in your hours | Convex — Fund II at $300–500M if Fund I is top-half |

---

## The honest take

**Expected value is roughly a wash in the mid-case (~$15M over 10 years).** The fund only dominates if you actually raise Fund II — and Fund II only happens if Fund I clears median, which is ~50/50 net of fees and survivorship.

**The fund is the right bet if:**
- You have LP relationships already (anchor LP for Fund I lined up)
- You can stomach $2M+ illiquid and 5 years of ambiguity
- You want a franchise, not a job — Fund III at $750M is the real prize
- You like the deal-sourcing / capital-allocation work more than the operating work

**Interim CEO is the right bet if:**
- You don't have anchor LPs and don't want to spend 18 months fundraising
- You want shorter feedback loops and the option to walk after each gig
- You're a better operator than allocator
- Optionality matters to you more than convexity

The asymmetry that's easy to miss: the fund path's downside isn't just $0, it's $0 *plus* the 10 years you can't get back *plus* the "couldn't raise Fund II" reputation. The interim path's downside is just a dry quarter.
